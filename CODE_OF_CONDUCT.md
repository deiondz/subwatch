# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on the work. Welcome questions, assume good intent, accept correction gracefully, and avoid personal attacks, harassment, or publishing another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile.
